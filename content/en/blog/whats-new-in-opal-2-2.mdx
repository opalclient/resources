Opal 2.2, the Remastered update, is out on Minecraft 26.2. The short version: theme the whole client around your accent colour, flip back to the old v1 look whenever you want, and let one module handle the right helpers for whatever minigame you load into. On top of that, a long list of modules came back from v1.

<KeyTakeaways>

- Your accent colour runs through the entire client, with a real theme editor and a colour wheel.
- The Opal v1 Preset flips the whole UI back to the v1 look in one click.
- One Minigames module reads your game and turns on the helpers that fit it.
- A new set of lag modules, plus combat and movement modules back from v1.

</KeyTakeaways>

## Theme the whole client

Your accent colour now runs through the entire client, not just the HUD. There is a proper theme editor in the Command Palette with a real colour wheel, so you are done pasting hex codes, and you can save the swatches you keep reaching for. You also get a UI font setting, with the classic Minecraft pixel font built in, and component presets that decide how the ClickGUI, HUD cards and settings rows are shaped.

## Flip back to the v1 look

Prefer how v1 looked? One preset brings it all back: arraylist, target HUD, watermarks, notifications and the old ClickGUI. Nothing to reconfigure, just switch the preset.

## Drag-and-drop overlay editor

Open the overlay editor from the Command Palette and drag or resize any HUD element with a live preview. Each element keeps its own settings behind a gear button, and your existing layout carries straight over.

## One module that knows your minigame

The new Minigames module reads the minigame you are in and turns on the helpers that fit it.

In Bed Wars it finds your bed, warns you when an enemy grabs obsidian or anything else worth tracking, watches milk timers, and stops the bed breaker from hitting your own bed. Open the shop and Auto Buy fills your hotbar in order: blocks first, then the best sword and armor you can afford, then tools, then tool upgrades once your armor is solid. Buy something yourself and it steps back, so fireballs and the rest are still your call.

Skywars gets enemy tracking and item alerts. Murder Mystery works out the murderer and detective, tints them in ESP, and keeps your aura off the innocents. Duels gives you Bridge info and an edge wall in Sumo. There is also auto requeue, a silent auto who pulled from tab, and an overlay card that shows what is happening in your current game.

## Bed Defender

Bind it, stand on your bed, tap the key. It stacks as many layers around the bed as your hotbar blocks allow, strongest blocks innermost, placed the same way Scaffold builds, then turns itself off.

## Lag modules

A new set of packet-timing tools:

- Backtrack hits players where they were a moment ago, with raytraced range and next-tick prediction.
- Fake Lag holds your packets back and flushes on its own when you shop, place, scaffold, chat, break blocks or take knockback.
- Stasis freezes you in place server-side until you let go.
- Displace handles knockback tricks, void mode included.
- Lag Range draws a ghost box where the server thinks a player is.

## Hit and kill effects

Hit Effects like SuperheroFX, bubbles, fireflies, petals, shards and pixel bursts now fire when you land a hit, and they trigger on arrows and other projectiles too. Kill Effects give you a bigger burst when a target drops, on players or mobs, and Damage Indicators show your damage in a clean new style.

## Modules back from v1, plus new ones

Triggerbot is new. Chams and Criticals are back. Anti Fireball now hits fireballs the way the aura hits players, with its own CPS and an optional pre-swing. The Disabler picks up a CubeCraft bypass. NoSlow has its NCP mode again, and Flight and Speed both get a new Rewound mode. Auto Head can eat golden and enchanted golden apples, the aura no longer treats ungrouped players as teammates, and Block works against players again.

## Alt manager and relations

The alt manager got a reworked UI, cookie file login, and the option to delete vaults you own. Relations moved into the Command Palette too, so you can add, switch and remove them there, with a bind to mark whoever you are looking at.

## Quality of life

- Repeated chat messages stack into one line with a count.
- Opal user tags stay inside the chat box.
- Right-click a ClickGUI category header to collapse it.
- Fixed the F5 camera, blur breaking after a resource pack reload, and a handful of HUD glitches.
